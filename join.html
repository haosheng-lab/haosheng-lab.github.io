Join us or collaborate

Help develop bioelectronics to investigate changing nervous systems and other living systems. We welcome students and researchers from electrical engineering, mechanics, materials science, computer science, neuroscience, biology, AI, and other disciplines who share these interests.

Research opportunities

Graduate students

Tell us which research questions interest you and how your previous experience connects to them. Include your CV and a brief description of a project you have worked on.

Formal applications and admission requirements are handled through Westlake University's graduate programs. Contacting the lab is a way to discuss research fit.

Westlake graduate admissions ↗

Postdoctoral researchers

Send your CV, a one-page research statement, contact details for three references, and your preferred start date.

Undergraduates and research interns

Share your interests, relevant coursework or project experience, and anticipated availability. A short description of something you have built, measured, or analyzed can help us find a suitable starting point.

Research staff and visiting researchers

Get in touch about potential technical roles or visits. Include your experience, interests, and preferred timing so we can discuss fit and availability.

Collaborate with us

We welcome collaboration across disciplines on questions, ideas, and projects related to our work. If you see a connection with your research or have an idea to explore together, please get in touch.
